## Co-op design principles
- Every character should contribute visibly and meaningfully.
- Each pairing should feel distinct, reflecting the characters' relationships and fighting styles.
- Moves must work in single-player with an AI/partner command only if that system is designed; do not assume local/online multiplayer exists. First milestone is single-player unless explicitly changed.
- Do not promise two-player multiplayer just because a co-op attack exists. Define co-op attacks as paired/team attacks with the actual partner-control mechanic to be decided.
- Boss interactions need fair telegraphs and tested boss-specific rules; do not assume every boss can be grabbed/launched.
- Preserve readable mobile controls; avoid requiring complicated simultaneous button chords unless an accessibility alternative exists.

## Romeo & Mei — trust-break scene (user-approved story beat, 2026-11-08)

**Required emotional beat:** Mei must clearly tell Romeo that she feels used and that she no longer trusts him. This is not a mild misunderstanding or playful annoyance. Romeo's initial deception—approaching her partly to get information about Coldman—has damaged her trust. He must earn it back through actions over time; one apology or flirtatious remark does not instantly repair the relationship.

**Draft dialogue for review (the emotional direction is approved; exact wording remains a draft):**

**Mei:** “So that's what this was? You used me to get to Coldman.”

**Romeo:** “It started that way. I won't pretend it didn't.”

**Mei:** “You made me think you actually cared. I trusted you, Romeo. Now I feel like I was just another way for you to get what you wanted.”

**Romeo:** “Mei, I—”

**Mei:** “Don't. I don't trust you anymore. If you want me to believe a word you say, you'll have to prove it. And don't expect me to make that easy.”

**Romeo:** “I understand. I won't ask you to trust me. I'll give you a reason to, if you'll let me.”

**Performance and continuity notes:**
- Mei should sound hurt and angry, not coy; leave a clear emotional pause after “I don't trust you anymore.”
- Romeo should not joke, flirt, or pressure her to forgive him in this scene.
- This establishes the slow-trust arc already recorded for their bonus storyline; Romeo earns trust through consistent choices and changed behavior.
- Place this beat in the later bonus-story stretch when Mei learns the truth about Romeo's original motive. Exact stage placement and final wording remain to be confirmed.
- Status: user-approved emotional requirement; dialogue text is a proposed draft, not yet line-by-line approved.

## Kanan + Jaylen — signature co-op concepts (2026-11-09)

**Character dynamic:** Kanan is the quiet, observant, silent-type fighter and steady leader. He communicates mostly through head nods, hand signals, and body language, speaking only occasionally. Jaylen is more expressive and energetic; his loyalty and familiarity with Kanan let him understand Kanan's signals without lengthy explanations.

**Stage partner selection:** Before a stage, the player chooses the lead character and chooses one partner from the available roster. The selected partner is controlled by AI. Each character should retain a distinct personality in their reactions, callouts, support behavior, and team attacks. This is a design direction; detailed AI behavior and implementation remain to be specified.

### Attack 1 — Silent Execution
- Pair: Kanan + Jaylen.
- Status: proposed concept; awaiting user line-by-line approval.
- Role: fast, tactical coordinated takedown, especially suited to regular enemies.
- Sequence: Kanan gives Jaylen a quick hand signal; Kanan rushes in with a stomach strike; the enemy staggers; Jaylen grabs and lifts the opponent; Kanan follows with a clean aerial kick; Jaylen completes the throwdown; both return to their fighting stances.
- Dialogue draft:
  - Jaylen: “You ready?”
  - Kanan: silent nod.
  - Jaylen: “That's all I needed.”
- Production: distinct character animation tracks, impact effects, sound cues, and synchronized timing required.

### Attack 2 — Brothers' Judgment
- Pair: Kanan + Jaylen.
- Status: proposed concept; awaiting user line-by-line approval.
- Role: slower, heavier attack intended as a boss-punishing team finisher, subject to boss-specific grab/launch rules and balance testing.
- Sequence: Kanan signals Jaylen to flank; Kanan attacks from the front with a punch combination; Jaylen rushes from behind and secures a waist grab; Kanan delivers a spinning kick to the opponent's upper body; Jaylen launches the enemy upward; Kanan catches the airborne opponent with a powerful follow-up strike; Jaylen finishes with a ground slam; both stand together as the enemy hits the ground.
- Dialogue draft:
  - Jaylen: “You always this quiet?”
  - Kanan: “Focus.”
  - Jaylen: “That's my brother!”
- Production: slower timing and stronger impact presentation than Silent Execution; distinct animation tracks, effects, and audio cues.

**Design distinction:** Silent Execution is the quicker tactical attack; Brothers' Judgment is the heavier boss-punish concept. Both are team attacks, not evidence that local or online multiplayer is implemented. The AI-partner activation method, input, resource cost, cooldown, damage values, and exact frame counts remain undecided.

**Approval record:** User approved the overall personality balance and concept direction (“Yes”) on 2026-11-09. Individual move names, exact choreography, and dialogue wording remain marked as proposed pending explicit approval.

### Between-stage team management

After each stage, show a **NEXT MOVE** / **TEAM SETUP** screen:
- Continue with the same team.
- Change Main Fighter.
- Change Partner.
- Change Both.
- Review the next stage briefing.

Changing the team does not reset campaign progress. Preserve completed stages, collected clues, mission outcomes, story flags, and each character's knowledge. The next stage's optional banter and partner reactions use the newly selected duo and current story state.

### First partner banter scene — Dockside District

**Placement:** Short pre-stage scene after the player confirms Kanan + Jaylen, before the first Dockside encounter.

**Visual staging:** Night at the docks. Rain taps against shipping containers. Kanan studies a warehouse entrance from behind stacked crates. Jaylen steps beside him, rolling his shoulders and looking toward the guards.

**Draft dialogue / action:**
- **Jaylen:** “Three at the door. Two more up on the walkway. You want me to make some noise?”
- Kanan raises two fingers, then points toward the walkway.
- **Jaylen:** “You take the high ground. I keep the front busy. Same as always.”
- Kanan gives a short nod.
- **Jaylen:** “Man, one day I'm getting you to use a whole sentence.”
- Kanan starts moving.
- **Jaylen:** “Yeah, yeah. I'm coming.”

**Gameplay handoff:** The player gains control as Kanan and Jaylen split toward their approach positions. The opening encounter teaches basic combat and partner support. When the player triggers the first paired attack opportunity, Kanan signals and Jaylen responds, introducing **Silent Execution** as the duo's signature coordinated takedown concept. The exact trigger/input and tutorial timing remain to be designed.

### Dialogue and continuity rules
- This exchange is specific to the Kanan + Jaylen pairing; other selected partners receive their own scene or a shorter variation.
- Kanan's silence is intentional characterization, not missing dialogue. Use readable gestures, eye-lines, and reactions.
- Do not play Kanan + Jaylen-only lines if a different partner is selected.
- Do not let optional banter reveal information a character has not learned yet.
- Keep scenes short enough to preserve the pace of a mobile beat-'em-up campaign.


## Kanan + Shai — romantic relationship and interaction direction (2026-11-09)

**User-confirmed canon:** Kanan and Shai are a couple / lovers. Their campaign interactions should be flirtatious, tense, and concerned for one another.

### Relationship performance
- Kanan remains quiet and communicates primarily through gestures, expressions, eye contact, and occasional short lines. His affection should be visible through attentive actions and subtle physical reassurance, not a sudden change into a talkative character.
- Shai knows Kanan well and can interpret his nonverbal signals. She can tease him, challenge his decisions, and recognize when he is hiding pain or worry.
- Their exchanges should balance flirtation, genuine concern, occasional friction, and affectionate familiarity.
- Their relationship should feel established and mutual, not like a first-date romance. Let intimacy show through trust, shorthand communication, and concern under pressure.
- Avoid making every exchange romantic. They are also capable fighters with their own priorities and personalities.

### Interaction beats to use across the campaign
1. **Pre-stage:** a brief teasing exchange or knowing look before they enter danger.
2. **During combat:** Shai notices when Kanan is exposed; Kanan instinctively covers her blind side. Keep combat callouts short.
3. **After combat:** one checks the other's injuries; relief may come out as irritation or a quiet touch.
4. **Disagreement:** one takes a reckless risk; the other calls it out because the fear underneath is real.
5. **Quiet moments:** shared looks, a hand on the shoulder, or a small private joke can communicate more than a long speech.

### Sample Dockside interaction — draft
**Placement:** Optional pre-stage scene when the player selects Kanan + Shai.

Shai catches Kanan checking the alley and then glances at the fresh scrape on his arm.

**Shai:** “You know, most people say hello before they run off bleeding.”

Kanan looks at the scrape, then back at her, as if it is nothing.

**Shai:** “Don't give me that look. I know you.”

Kanan reaches for her hand briefly, then points toward the warehouse entrance.

**Shai:** “Work first. Lecture later?”

Kanan gives a small nod.

**Shai:** “Good. And try not to make me worry this time.”

Kanan pauses, meets her eyes, and gives her hand one reassuring squeeze before moving out.

### Continuity guardrails
- Kanan + Shai scenes are distinct from Kanan + Jaylen's brotherly banter.
- If the player selects Shai with another partner, do not play Kanan/Shai romantic scenes.
- If Kanan and Shai are both present, their relationship may inform optional banter and support animations, while the main plot remains coherent for other team combinations.
- The campaign should not assume the player always chooses them together; preserve their relationship in story continuity even when the player selects a different partner.
