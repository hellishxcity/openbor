# Streets of Violence — Story So Far
Updated: 2026-11-09
Status: consolidated story outline from recovered project notes. Confirmed facts are separated from connective story details that still need to be written.

## Premise and central conflict
Streets of Violence follows five fighters whose lives and loyalties intersect as they take on the criminal empire controlled by Ali, known as Kingpin. The campaign moves through the city's districts and criminal strongholds, building toward the King's Fall and the confrontation with Ali. Jenni Rows is an undercover police officer infiltrating the organization. Her cousin Mei Lin is also undercover inside the gang, but Jenni does not know this during the main campaign's Stage 8 encounter.

**Important limitation:** The complete opening inciting incident, each hero's personal motivation, the precise sequence of events connecting all ten stages, and the full final-boss resolution have not yet been recovered in enough detail to present as established canon. This document does not invent those missing events.

## Main playable team
- **Kanan “Scorpion” Jaxon** — fast, balanced martial artist; signature Scorpion Strike.
- **Jaylen “Coldman” Jaxon** — Kanan's older brother; powerful wrestler and street brawler.
- **Shai Hooks** — Kanan's fiancée; kickboxer and tech-savvy fighter.
- **Jenni “Legs” Rows** — taekwondo specialist and undercover police officer working to infiltrate Kingpin's organization.
- **Nona “Beauty” Silks** — MMA/grappler; former criminal recruited by Jenni.

The five are available from the beginning of Story Mode. The exact point at which each joins the narrative and the full personal arcs still need recovery.

## Main campaign — ten districts
The established campaign order is:
1. **Dockside District** — the opening waterfront district, with shipping containers, warehouses, loading yards and piers. The route includes alternate paths through the Container Maze or Warehouse Entrance, then Waterfront Walk or Service Tunnel, before reconnecting at Brick's boss pier.
2. **The Concrete Jungle**
3. **The Ironworks**
4. **Neon Strip**
5. **Lockdown**
6. **Underground**
7. **King's Territory**
8. **The Shipyard**
9. **No Man's Land**
10. **The King's Fall** — campaign finale, culminating in the confrontation with Ali/Kingpin.

Known Dockside encounters include Dockside Grunts, Street Vipers and Iron Guards. Brock is a heavy bruiser mini-boss; he is not Brick. Brick is the Stage 1 main boss and returns in Stages 7 and 10.

Other named bosses across the planned campaign include Razor, The Butcher, Money Man, Captain Cross, Viper Queen, Iron Fist, DJ Havoc and Warden Stone. Ali/Kingpin is the final boss. Exact stage-to-boss assignments beyond Brick's known appearances are not all confirmed in the recovered register.

## Jenni, Mei, and the undercover secret
Jenni Rows is an undercover police officer infiltrating Kingpin's gang. Mei Lin is Jenni's younger cousin and is independently undercover inside the gang. Jenni is not supposed to know Mei is there during the main story's Stage 8 encounter.

### Main Story — Stage 8: The Shipyard
- Mei can be discovered and fought only if the player is using **Shai Hooks or Nona “Beauty” Silks**.
- Jenni is absent from this encounter and remains unaware of Mei's identity.
- The fight between Mei and the selected character ends in a draw.
- Mei escapes when the others arrive, keeping her identity secret from Jenni.
- The encounter must not reveal Mei to Jenni prematurely.

### Bonus Story — Stage 21
Mei's identity is revealed to Jenni in Bonus Stage 21. The exact reveal scene, dialogue, and Jenni's immediate reaction still need to be written/recovered.

## Romeo Wells and Mei Lin — bonus storyline
Romeo Wells is a mercenary hired by Ali/Kingpin before Ali's arrest to kill Jaylen “Coldman” Jaxon, because Coldman threatened Ali's criminal empire. Romeo is a secret playable character after the main Story Mode is beaten.

Mei Lin is also a secret playable character, unlocked after the player beats the main Story Mode. Their unlocks are not earned simply by completing their bonus stages.

### Bonus Stage 2 — their first meeting
Romeo and Mei are complete strangers when they meet as opposing bosses. After their confrontation, they compliment each other's fighting ability and agree to stop the criminal operation. Their first meeting must remain their first meeting; do not write them as already knowing each other beforehand.

Recovered dialogue from the encounter:
- **Romeo:** “You're pretty good. I wasn't expecting that.”
- **Mei:** “And you're not half bad yourself. But you're fighting for the wrong people.”
- **Romeo:** “Maybe it's time I changed that.”
- **Mei:** “Then let's take this whole operation down.”
- **Romeo:** “Now that's something I can agree with.”

### The trust problem
Romeo initially approaches Mei for two reasons: he thinks she is cute, and he wants information about Coldman. His concealed motive creates a real breach of trust. Mei must explicitly express that she feels used and no longer trusts him. The emotional direction is approved; the following wording is a draft saved for review:

**Mei:** “So that's what this was? You used me to get to Coldman.”

**Romeo:** “It started that way. I won't pretend it didn't.”

**Mei:** “You made me think you actually cared. I trusted you, Romeo. Now I feel like I was just another way for you to get what you wanted.”

**Romeo:** “Mei, I—”

**Mei:** “Don't. I don't trust you anymore. If you want me to believe a word you say, you'll have to prove it. And don't expect me to make that easy.”

**Romeo:** “I understand. I won't ask you to trust me. I'll give you a reason to, if you'll let me.”

Mei is genuinely hurt and angry, not coy or playfully jealous. Romeo must not pressure her to forgive him; he has to rebuild trust through consistent actions over time.

### Bonus Stage 19
Mei is annoyed with Romeo and makes him earn her trust. This is a slow-burn trust arc, not an instant romance or immediate reconciliation.

### Bonus Stage 20 — Coldman's confrontation with Romeo
Coldman defeats Romeo. Romeo learns the truth about Coldman, accepts defeat, voids the contract, keeps half of the payment, and stops hunting him. Romeo survives; this is not a death scene.

### Bonus Stage 21 — Mei's identity reveal
Mei's undercover identity is revealed to Jenni. This happens in the bonus story, not during main Story Stage 8.

### Bonus storyline range
The bonus storyline expands through Stages 16–24. Stage 2 is also identified in recovered notes as Romeo and Mei's first meeting. The exact event-by-event sequence and complete dialogue for Stages 16–24 are not yet fully recovered; stage numbers and scene chronology need a final continuity pass before scripting.

## Secret character unlocks
Romeo Wells and Mei Lin are both secret playable characters. Both unlock only after the player beats/completes the main game (Story Mode). They are not in the starting roster. Whether they unlock simultaneously or independently has not been separately specified.

## Character-specific secret missions
Each of the five starting fighters has three optional character-specific secret missions (15 total). They can appear on eligible stages according to the selected character and route. These are optional side stories and encounters; they do not block completion of the main campaign.

- **Kanan:** The Old Man's Last Coin; Red Ledger; No One Left Behind.
- **Jaylen:** Wrong Turn Ambush; Heavy Debt; Brother's Trail.
- **Shai:** Safe Signal; The Stolen Drive; One More Round.
- **Jenni:** Get Them Out; Evidence Locker; Cover Not Blown.
- **Nona:** Old Gym, New Trouble; Debt Collector's Lesson; No One Gets Left Behind.

These mission titles and prototypes are documented, but the complete dialogue and their placement in the main plot remain to be developed.
