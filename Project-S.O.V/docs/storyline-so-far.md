# Streets of Violence — Story So Far
Updated: 2026-11-09
Status: consolidated story outline from recovered project notes. Confirmed facts are separated from connective story details that still need to be written.

## Premise and central conflict
Streets of Violence follows five fighters whose lives and loyalties intersect as they take on the criminal empire controlled by Ali, known as Kingpin. The campaign moves through the city's districts and criminal strongholds, building toward the King's Fall and the confrontation with Ali. Jenni Rows is an undercover police officer infiltrating the organization. Her cousin Mei Lin is also undercover inside the gang, but Jenni does not know this during the main campaign's Stage 8 encounter.

**Important limitation:** The complete opening inciting incident, each hero's personal motivation, the precise sequence of events connecting all ten stages, and the full final-boss resolution have not yet been recovered in enough detail to present as established canon. This document does not invent those missing events.

## Main playable team
- **Kanan “Scorpion” Jaxon** — fast, balanced martial artist; signature Scorpion Strike.
- **Jaylen “Coldman” Jaxon** — Kanan's older brother; powerful wrestler and street brawler.
- **Shai Hooks** — Kanan's fiancée; kickboxer and tech-savvy fighter.
- **Jenni “Legs” Rows** — taekwondo specialist and undercover police officer working to infiltrate Kingpin's organization.
- **Nona “Beauty” Silks** — MMA/grappler; former criminal recruited by Jenni.

The five are available from the beginning of Story Mode. The exact point at which each joins the narrative and the full personal arcs still need recovery.

## Main campaign — ten districts
The established campaign order is:
1. **Dockside District** — the opening waterfront district, with shipping containers, warehouses, loading yards and piers. The route includes alternate paths through the Container Maze or Warehouse Entrance, then Waterfront Walk or Service Tunnel, before reconnecting at Brick's boss pier.
2. **The Concrete Jungle**
3. **The Ironworks**
4. **Neon Strip**
5. **Lockdown**
6. **Underground**
7. **King's Territory**
8. **The Shipyard**
9. **No Man's Land**
10. **The King's Fall** — campaign finale, culminating in the confrontation with Ali/Kingpin.

Known Dockside encounters include Dockside Grunts, Street Vipers and Iron Guards. Brock is a heavy bruiser mini-boss; he is not Brick. Brick is the Stage 1 main boss and returns in Stages 7 and 10.

Other named bosses across the planned campaign include Razor, The Butcher, Money Man, Captain Cross, Viper Queen, Iron Fist, DJ Havoc and Warden Stone. Ali/Kingpin is the final boss. Exact stage-to-boss assignments beyond Brick's known appearances are not all confirmed in the recovered register.

Jenni Rows is an undercover police officer infiltrating Kingpin's gang. Mei Lin is Jenni's younger cousin and is independently undercover inside the gang. Jenni is not supposed to know Mei is there during the main story's Stage 8 encounter.

### Bonus Stage 2 — their first meeting

Their first meeting must remain their first meeting; do not write them as already knowing each other beforehand.

### The trust problem
Romeo initially approaches Mei for two reasons: he thinks she is cute, and he wants information about Coldman. His concealed motive creates a real breach of trust. Mei must explicitly express that she feels used and no longer trusts him. The emotional direction is approved; the following wording is a draft saved for review:

## Stage 1 — Dockside District: the ledger clue and secret-mission hooks (draft, 2026-11-09)

**Status:** New connective story material drafted for review. It builds on the established Stage 1 setting, Brick's role as the main boss, and the already-listed character secret missions. The exact clue wording, scene dialogue, and mission objectives are not yet locked canon.

### After the fight — the ledger

The gang collector drops a weather-stained ledger as Kanan and his partner finish the encounter. Kanan picks it up and flips through pages filled with dock numbers, shipment dates, protection payments, and initials. Most entries look like ordinary gang bookkeeping until he notices the same unusual notation repeated beside several shipments:

**“U-6 / NIGHT TRANSFER / NO SURFACE MANIFEST.”**

A rough hand-drawn mark in the margin resembles a maintenance access symbol. A second note reads:

**“Move it below street level. Keep the dock crews quiet.”**

The ledger does not explain what is being moved or who ordered it. It does establish that Ali's operation is using the docks to move something through an unlisted route beneath the city. Kanan keeps the ledger as evidence. The next stage follows the money and names in the book into the city's wider criminal network; the “U-6” notation becomes a clue that can be recognized again when the campaign reaches **Stage 6 — Underground**.

### Partner-specific version: Kanan + Jaylen

Jaylen wipes his hands and notices the ledger.

**Jaylen:** “Please tell me that's the list of people who owe us money.”

Kanan turns the book so Jaylen can see the repeated U-6 notation.

**Jaylen:** “U-6? That's not a dock number I've ever seen.”

Kanan taps the words “below street level.”

**Jaylen:** “So whatever they're moving, they're hiding it under the city. Great. Just what we needed.”

Kanan closes the ledger and gives Jaylen a short nod.

**Jaylen:** “Right. We keep this. And next time, let me know before you go collecting evidence with your face.”

Kanan gives him a dry look. Jaylen grins, then follows him out.

### Partner-specific version: Kanan + Shai

Shai steps beside Kanan as he studies the ledger. She notices the repeated U-6 notation and takes a closer look.

**Shai:** “That's not a shipping code. Not one the port uses, anyway.”

Kanan points to “below street level.”

**Shai:** “A route underneath the docks? That's why the records don't match.”

Kanan looks toward her, waiting.

**Shai:** “Don't worry. I can trace the code. But you're not going down there alone.”

Kanan gives her a small, reassuring nod.

**Shai:** “That wasn't a request, Kanan.”

He almost smiles. She folds the page carefully and hands the ledger back to him.

### Secret-mission hooks

These optional hooks branch from the same Stage 1 aftermath. They do not replace the main story clue, and the player can complete the main campaign without doing them.

#### Kanan — “Red Ledger”
A frightened dock clerk quietly approaches Kanan after recognizing the ledger. He says the collector was not carrying the only copy: a second, red-bound account book is being taken to a nearby gang safe point before dawn. It contains names of residents being charged protection money and records of payments to the gang.

**Draft objective:** Find the gang courier, recover the red-bound ledger, and escape before reinforcements seal the route.

**Story purpose:** Give Kanan a personal, evidence-driven mission that shows his quiet protectiveness. The recovered names can later help the fighters identify people being targeted by Ali's organization.

#### Shai — “Safe Signal”
A dockworker approaches Shai because the gang has confiscated a small encrypted radio relay used by the dock crew to warn one another when gang enforcers arrive. Without it, several workers are trapped in a sealed loading area and cannot call for help. Shai recognizes that the relay can be restored if she retrieves its missing power cell and reconnects it.

**Draft objective:** Infiltrate the nearby container lane, recover the relay's power cell, reactivate the signal, and guide the trapped workers to safety.

**Story purpose:** Highlight Shai's technical skills and concern for ordinary people. The relay's recovered signal log may contain a brief transmission mentioning the same “U-6” route, reinforcing the main clue without giving away the Underground chapter early.

### Continuity and gameplay notes
- The main ledger discovery occurs regardless of which partner the player selected.
- Partner-specific dialogue changes to match the selected duo; do not play Jaylen's or Shai's version when that character is absent.
- Kanan's “Red Ledger” and Shai's “Safe Signal” are already established secret-mission titles; these are proposed Stage 1 hooks and objectives, pending user approval.
- Completing either secret mission is optional and should not be required to understand the main campaign.
- Keep the meaning of “U-6” partly unresolved until Stage 6 — Underground.
- Do not imply that the ledger alone proves who Ali's suppliers are; it is a lead for further investigation.


### Shai's Stage 1 secret mission — “Safe Signal” (expanded draft, 2026-11-09)

**Status:** User approved connecting the ledger's forced-worker evidence to a personal rescue mission for Shai. This mission sketch is a draft; Jay Brooks is the rescued person, while exact dialogue and the full nature of her history with Shai remain open for refinement.

**Trigger:** Available after the Stage 1 Dockside ledger discovery if Shai is selected as the main fighter or partner. The ledger contains a name Shai recognizes beside repeated protection-payment entries and a coded reference to the old loading station.

**Personal connection:** The name is Jay Brooks, a dockworker Shai knows and cares about. Jay has quietly helped local workers stay safe from gang pressure and has been taken along with several other workers after refusing to cooperate with Ali's operation. Jay is established as a recurring supporting ally, with her next major appearance planned for Stage 6 — Underground.

#### Opening scene
Shai studies the ledger and stops at a familiar name.

**Shai:** “I know that name. They wouldn't be on this list unless something happened.”

Kanan looks from her to the ledger.

**Shai:** “They're holding people somewhere near the old loading station. If we wait, those workers could disappear.”

Kanan closes the ledger and nods.

**Shai:** “I knew you'd understand. Let's get them out.”

Kanan gestures toward the exit. Shai gives him a determined look and follows.

#### Mission objectives
1. Reach the old loading station using the route marked by the ledger.
2. Disable the gang's signal jammer / alarm relay so the captives can call for help.
3. Defeat the guards and locate the holding area.
4. Free the dockworkers, including Shai's acquaintance.
5. Escort the workers through the service lane to a safe exit.

#### Gameplay beats
- **Approach:** A short container-yard combat encounter introduces guards watching the loading station.
- **Signal puzzle:** Shai uses her technical skills to identify and disable the jammer. Keep the interaction simple and readable for mobile controls; it should be a brief action under pressure, not a long puzzle.
- **Rescue:** The player fights through the holding area. One guard attempts to trigger the alarm, creating a short timed interruption.
- **Escape:** The rescued workers move toward the exit while the player protects the route from a final wave of enemies.
- **Completion:** Shai's acquaintance thanks her. Shai tries to keep her composure, but the relief is obvious. Kanan stays close and gives her a quiet, reassuring touch.

#### Story payoff
- The rescued workers confirm that Ali's gang is forcing people to work, collecting protection payments, and moving shipments through routes that do not appear on official dock records.
- The disabled relay yields a partial signal log containing the same **U-6** notation found in the ledger. This reinforces the main mystery without revealing the full Underground operation before Stage 6.
- The rescue gives Shai a personal reason to keep investigating Ali's network.
- Completing the mission is optional. The main campaign still advances if the player skips it; the ledger remains the core clue.

#### Partner variations
- **Kanan present:** He notices Shai's reaction before she explains. Their established romantic bond is conveyed through a quiet nod, staying beside her, and a reassuring gesture. Do not make him suddenly talkative.
- **Another partner present:** Shai still reacts personally to the ledger clue if she is selected as the lead. The accompanying character supports her in their own established voice; do not play Kanan/Shai romantic beats unless Kanan is actually present.
- **Shai absent:** The main ledger discovery still happens, but this Shai-specific optional mission does not trigger.

#### Continuity and open decisions
- This mission expands the already-established Shai secret-mission title **“Safe Signal”**; it does not replace it.
- Jay Brooks is the rescued person and is planned to return as a recurring supporting ally, with her next major appearance in Stage 6 — Underground. The finer details of her history with Shai and her later appearances remain open.
- The exact mission map, enemy roster, timer values, rewards, and OpenBOR implementation are not yet designed.
- Keep the rescued person's identity consistent if they return in later stages.


## Stage 6 — Underground: Jay Brooks returns (story outline draft, 2026-11-09)

**Status:** This is the proposed Stage 6 return for Jay Brooks, following the user's approval to make her a recurring supporting ally after Shai's optional Stage 1 secret mission, “Safe Signal.” The broad role and story connection are approved; exact dialogue, level layout, encounter order, and gameplay implementation remain drafts.

### Why Jay returns

Since the Dockside rescue, Jay has quietly kept in touch with Shai and continued gathering information from dockworkers and other residents. She has compared the U-6 notation from the shipment records with old freight schedules and maintenance maps. She discovers that U-6 refers to a network of sealed service passages beneath the city—not one single tunnel, but a route connecting several old utility and freight access points.

Jay does not become a fighter or a permanent member of the playable roster. Her value is her local knowledge, her courage, and the trust she has built among workers who are afraid to speak openly about Ali's operation.

### Stage opening — the signal from below

As the team investigates the U-6 lead, Shai receives a brief message from Jay. Jay has found a maintenance access point beneath an abandoned transit depot. The message cuts off after she warns that gang guards have begun moving people and equipment through the passage.

If Shai completed “Safe Signal,” the exchange carries their established friendship and relief at hearing from one another again. If the player skipped that optional mission, Jay's appearance must still make sense: she can be introduced through the dockworker network and the evidence connected to the Stage 1 ledger, without assuming the player personally rescued her.

**Draft exchange (if Shai is present):**

**Shai:** “Jay? I thought you were staying clear of the docks.”

**Jay:** “I was. Then I found where those shipments go. U-6 isn't a warehouse code. It's a whole route under the city.”

**Shai:** “You should've waited for us.”

**Jay:** “And let them move everyone before you got here? You know me better than that.”

Shai is concerned, but recognizes that Jay has brought them the lead they need. Kanan gives Jay a respectful nod.

### Stage route and objectives

1. **Find the access point:** Fight through the abandoned transit depot and locate the maintenance entrance marked on Jay's map.
2. **Break the surface security:** Defeat the gang guards protecting the access lift and disable the lockout control.
3. **Enter the U-6 network:** Explore connected freight tunnels, utility corridors, and underground loading platforms. Environmental clues show that the route has been used to move shipments and hold unwilling workers out of public view.
4. **Recover the transfer records:** Reach a guarded control room and retrieve the missing section of the U-6 manifest. The records connect multiple surface locations to the underground network, but do not yet reveal every person or organization behind Ali.
5. **Stop the transfer:** Intercept a shipment convoy or forced-worker transfer before it can leave the area. The player fights the guards and a heavy enforcer assigned to protect the operation.
6. **Get the workers out:** Open a secondary service exit and protect the rescued workers while they reach a safe route back to the surface.

### Jay's role during the mission

Jay stays near a secured communications point rather than joining the combat as a playable or AI fighter. She:
- guides the team through the passages using her maintenance-map knowledge;
- identifies old freight markings and explains which routes are still active;
- helps open or redirect a service gate after the team secures its control panel;
- contacts trusted workers to coordinate a safe evacuation once the route is clear.

Keep her exposed to danger and emotionally involved, but do not turn her into an implausibly capable action hero. The fighters handle the combat; Jay makes the rescue and investigation possible.

### Mid-mission complication

The gang begins sealing the tunnels after realizing the team has reached the control room. Jay discovers that one of the gates can only be held open manually from a nearby service station. She volunteers to reach it, but Shai insists that Jay wait until the immediate area is secure. This creates urgency without making Jay reckless or requiring her to fight.

The team clears the station. Jay operates the manual release, allowing the workers and the fighters to escape before the passage is sealed. If Shai is present, she and Jay share a brief moment of relief once everyone is through.

### Stage payoff

The recovered manifest confirms that U-6 is a hidden underground freight-and-transfer network used by Ali's organization. It gives the team concrete locations to investigate in later stages and proves that the Dockside ledger was an early trace of a much larger operation.

Jay's information also helps the fighters identify additional workers who need protection. She returns to the surface network to coordinate their safe relocation and promises to pass along any new evidence.

**Closing beat (draft):**

**Jay:** “You found the route. I can help get the people out, but there are more names on those records.”

**Shai:** “Then we'll keep going until we get them all.”

Jay looks to Kanan.

**Jay:** “Take care of her.”

Kanan gives a firm nod.

**Shai:** “I can take care of myself.”

**Jay:** “I know. That's why I said it to him.”

Shai smiles despite herself. The team moves on with the manifest.
